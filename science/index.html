Two Big Ideas

The whole unit is just these two sentences.

1.1 — every part has a job

Different parts do different jobs

Animals have many parts: legs, fins, wings, tails, teeth, tongues and beaks. Each part has a function. A function is the job that a part does.

The animal uses its part to do something.
📌 Almost every sentence in the textbook has this shape. Learn it once and your report writes itself.
1.2 — the shape explains the job

The shape fits the function

Parts come in different shapes. The shape is never an accident. It helps the animal move efficiently or obtain food.

The long, sharp beak helps it catch fish.
📌 This is where the marks are. "It has a tail" is level 1. "It has a long, thick tail, so it can wrap it around its body to keep warm" is level 5.
